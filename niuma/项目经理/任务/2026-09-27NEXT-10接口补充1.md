## D到B的真实接口

- pub(crate) ToolRuntime::with_files_control<R>(&self, f: impl FnOnce(&mut FilesControl<'_>)->anyhow::Result<R>)->Result<R>。
- FilesControl::snapshot(&self)->FilesRuntimeStatus；reconcile(&mut self, enabled:bool)->FilesRuntimeStatus。生命周期失败体现在failed/admission=false/recovery，不用Err掩盖已commit持久事实；外层锁故障或Store错误仍走Result。
- pub ToolRuntime::builtin_with_enabled(bool)->Result<Self>，旧builtin/disable/unload兼容入口保持。
- FilesRuntimeStatus字段state:FilesRuntimeState(active/stopped/unloaded/failed)，admission_open:bool，in_flight:u64，draining:bool，instances:Vec<FilesInstanceStatus>，providers:Vec<FilesProviderStatus>，effects_count/subscriptions_count:u64，recovery:Vec<FilesRecoveryStatus>。
- FilesInstanceStatus={plugin_id:String,state:FilesInstanceState,failure_stage:Option<FilesFailureStage>}。instance state额外容纳registered/loaded，failure stage activate/deactivate/deliver。
- FilesProviderStatus={key:InterfaceKey,provider_id:String}。FilesRecoveryStatus={plugin_id:String,stage:FilesFailureStage,cause_code:FilesFailureCauseCode}，cause code plugin_error/panic/provides_not_covered。所有DTO serde双向、snake_case枚举；版本与key沿现有InterfaceKey实际结构。

false初始化若没有实例记录，DTO仍显式列provider与consumer为unloaded，不用空数组冒充生命周期观察。过去recovery不抹去，但显式新协调成功后current可active，不因历史条目永远失败。D直接通知B/C精确字段；真实组件依赖由经理合，不写stub。
